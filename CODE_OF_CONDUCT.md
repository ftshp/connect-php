# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Scope and enforcement

The code of conduct applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to rastislav.nemec@icloud.com. Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected. The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 describes the standards and the enforcement guidelines that apply.
